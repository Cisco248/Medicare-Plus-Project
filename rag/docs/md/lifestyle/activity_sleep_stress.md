# Lifestyle and Human Activity Knowledge

## Physical activity

Physical activity includes all body movement that increases energy expenditure. Exercise is structured physical activity intended to improve fitness.

Useful categories:

- Daily movement
- Aerobic exercise
- Resistance training
- Balance training
- Flexibility
- Recreational activity

## Sedentary behavior

Sedentary behavior involves prolonged low-energy activity, commonly sitting or reclining while awake.

For cardiometabolic health, it is useful to:

- Reduce prolonged sitting
- Take movement breaks
- Increase daily walking
- Add structured activity according to ability

## Sleep

Sleep affects appetite, energy, mood and daily behavior. Poor or insufficient sleep can make healthy activity and eating patterns more difficult.

Sleep information from consumer devices should be treated as an estimate, not a medical diagnosis.

## Stress

Stress can affect behavior, sleep, food choices and physiological responses. Stress management may support adherence to healthy behaviors.

Possible approaches:

- Relaxation techniques
- Regular physical activity
- Social support
- Structured routines
- Professional mental-health support when needed

## Weight

Weight is one health indicator and should not be used alone to judge health. Appropriate weight management may improve diabetes, hypertension and cardiovascular risk factors.

## RAG activity interpretation

The system can identify patterns such as:

- Decreasing weekly activity
- Increasing sedentary time
- Reduced sleep duration
- Weight trend changes
- Changes in exercise consistency

These patterns should be presented as possible contributors or associations, not as definite causes.
