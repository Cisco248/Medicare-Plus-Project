# Exercise Knowledge Across Conditions

Walking, aerobic activity, and muscle strengthening support diabetes, blood pressure, and heart health when a clinician agrees. WHO adults: 150–300 minutes moderate or 75–150 minutes vigorous aerobic activity weekly, plus strengthening on two or more days, if able. Sedentary time should be reduced when possible. Start gradually after illness. Chest pain, severe breathlessness, or recurrent hypoglycaemia need individualized advice. This is not a personal exercise prescription.
