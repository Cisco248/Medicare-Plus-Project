# Medication Adherence

Taking medicines as prescribed, at the right times, with a refill plan, supports long-term control of diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease. Missing doses can raise glucose or blood pressure. Extra doses can be dangerous. If a dose is missed, follow the care team’s existing instruction rather than doubling by default. Side effects should be reported to a clinician. Cost and access problems deserve a clinical or pharmacy conversation, not abrupt cessation.

The three prediction models do not include adherence features.
