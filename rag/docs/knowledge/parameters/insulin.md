# Parameter: insulin

Insulin is not a numeric feature in the MediCare Plus diabetes, hypertension, or heart-disease models. Patients still ask about it because it is central to diabetes biology.

## Question: What does insulin mean?

### Answer

Insulin is a hormone made by the pancreas that helps glucose enter cells. Too little insulin, or poor insulin action (insulin resistance), leads to high blood glucose.

## Question: Why is insulin relevant?

### Answer

Type 1 diabetes involves severe insulin deficiency. Type 2 diabetes involves insulin resistance and often a later insulin shortage. Insulin medicine is required in type 1 diabetes and is used in some type 2 and pregnancy settings when prescribed.

## Model usage

The e-doc forms do not collect insulin dose. Do not infer insulin use from glucose or BMI.
