# Parameter: diastolic_bp

MediCare Plus diabetes model feature: `diastolic_bp`. Numeric. Unit: mmHg. Encoded: none. Scaled: yes, with the diabetes scaler.

## What is diastolic blood pressure?

Diastolic pressure is the lower number in a reading such as 120/80. It is the pressure when the heart relaxes between beats.

## Why it matters

Diastolic values of 80–89 mmHg fall in AHA educational stage 1 hypertension when systolic is not already in a higher band. Diastolic 90 or higher is stage 2 in that educational chart. Isolated high diastolic values still need clinical measurement technique and repeat readings.

## Model usage

The diabetes form stores diastolic from the 120/80 string. Both systolic and diastolic must be present.
