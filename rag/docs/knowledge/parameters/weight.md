# Parameter: weight

Hypertension e-doc field: `weight` in kilograms. Combined with height to compute BMI for the hypertension model. Diabetes and heart-disease BMI come from the clinical snapshot (profile or Health Connect).

## What is weight?

Body weight is mass in kilograms. Rapid unintended change can be a clinical concern independent of BMI category. Weight is not a glucose test.

## Lifestyle

Eating pattern and activity can influence weight over time. Crash diets are not recommended by this knowledge base.
