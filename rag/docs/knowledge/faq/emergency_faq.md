# Emergency FAQ

## What should I do if someone with diabetes becomes unconscious?

Call emergency services. Do not put food or drink in their mouth.

## What should I do if I have severe chest pain?

Call emergency services. Do not wait for an e-doc result.

## What should I do if my blood pressure is extremely high?

Crisis-range readings with severe symptoms need emergency care. Without symptoms, use good technique and seek prompt clinical advice.

## What are heart attack warning signs?

Chest pressure, shortness of breath, pain in arm, neck, jaw, or back, cold sweat, nausea, faintness.

## What are stroke warning signs?

Sudden face droop, arm weakness, speech difficulty, severe confusion, or sudden vision loss. Call emergency services.

## What should I do if I have a seizure?

Protect from injury, call emergency services, do not put objects in the mouth.
