# Heart Disease FAQ

## What is heart disease?

Heart disease is a group of conditions affecting the heart, including coronary artery disease, heart attack, heart failure, arrhythmias, valve disease, and cardiomyopathy. Also called cardiac disease, heart condition, cardiovascular disease.

## What causes heart disease?

Atherosclerosis, high blood pressure, diabetes, smoking, unfavorable lipids, family history, inactivity, and other factors can contribute. Cause in one person needs clinical assessment.

## What are heart attack warning signs?

Chest pain or pressure, pain in arm, jaw, or back, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea, or light-headedness. Women may have less classic symptoms. Call emergency services.

## How can I protect my heart?

Do not smoke, be active as able, follow a heart-healthy eating pattern, manage blood pressure, glucose, and cholesterol with a clinician, and take prescribed medicines.

## What parameters does the heart-disease model use?

Age category, sex, BMI, general health, diabetic survey status, smoking, stroke, difficulty walking, physical-health days.

## What does the prediction mean? Is it a diagnosis?

`High heart-disease risk` or `Lower heart-disease risk` from probability versus threshold (default 0.6). Not a diagnosis.

## Can I exercise?

Many people can, after clinician advice. Chest pain on exertion needs medical review, not a harder workout.
