# Sleep and Chronic Disease

## Sleep Data

The application may receive:

- sleep duration
- sleep start
- sleep end
- sleep consistency
- sleep interruptions

## Health Relationship

Poor sleep can be associated with:

- metabolic health problems
- cardiovascular risk
- blood-pressure problems
- glucose regulation problems

## RAG Rule

One short night:

Not enough evidence.

Repeated poor sleep:

Potential lifestyle/health concern.

## Important

Wearable sleep measurements are estimates.

They should not be used alone to diagnose:

- insomnia
- sleep apnea
- other sleep disorders
