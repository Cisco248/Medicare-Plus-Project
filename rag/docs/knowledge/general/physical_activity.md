# Physical Activity and Health

## Definition

Physical activity includes movement performed during daily life,
transportation, work and leisure.

## Health Benefits

Regular physical activity supports:

- cardiovascular health
- metabolic health
- weight management
- physical fitness
- mental wellbeing

## RAG Data

Useful data includes:

- steps
- active minutes
- intensity
- distance
- sedentary time
- weekly frequency

## Interpretation

Use longitudinal patterns whenever possible.

Single-day observations have limited clinical meaning.
