# Emergency Warning Signs

## Purpose

This document has high priority during RAG retrieval.

## Potential Emergency Symptoms

The system should recognize symptoms such as:

- severe chest pain or pressure
- severe breathing difficulty
- fainting
- loss of consciousness
- sudden weakness
- sudden numbness
- difficulty speaking
- severe confusion
- sudden severe vision problems
- seizure
- rapidly worsening severe symptoms

## RAG Behaviour

If emergency symptoms are detected:

DO NOT:

- generate normal lifestyle advice
- wait for another day's data
- diagnose the condition
- recommend medication changes

DO:

- clearly recommend urgent medical assessment
- keep the response concise
- prioritize safety
