# Sedentary Behaviour

## Definition

Sedentary behaviour refers to waking behaviour involving very low energy
expenditure while sitting, reclining or lying.

## RAG Interpretation

Distinguish:

Low steps
from

High sedentary time
from

Missing activity data.

These concepts are not interchangeable.

## Longitudinal Analysis

Repeated sedentary patterns are more meaningful than one inactive day.

## Output

Good:

"The patient has shown increased sedentary behaviour compared with the
previous 14-day period."

Avoid:

"The patient has a disease because of sedentary behaviour."
