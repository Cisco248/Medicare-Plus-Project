# General Exercise Guidelines

## Adults

General WHO guidance supports:

- 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week

OR

- 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity per week

OR

- an equivalent combination.

Muscle-strengthening activity is also recommended on at least two days
per week.

## Important

These are general population guidelines.

The application must not automatically prescribe an exercise plan.

Patients with:

- cardiovascular disease
- diabetes complications
- mobility limitations
- acute illness
- other medical conditions

may require individualized advice.
