# Diabetes Risk Factors

## Important Risk Factors

Risk factors associated with type 2 diabetes can include:

- overweight or obesity
- insufficient physical activity
- unhealthy dietary patterns
- family history
- increasing age
- previous metabolic abnormalities
- hypertension
- abnormal lipid levels

## Activity Relationship

Repeated physical inactivity may contribute to increased metabolic risk.

However:

Low steps != diabetes

Low activity != diagnosis

## RAG Rule

The system should describe these as risk factors rather than diagnoses.

## Example

Bad:

"The patient has diabetes because they walked 2000 steps."

Good:

"The patient's recent activity level is relatively low. Reduced physical
activity can be relevant to metabolic health and diabetes risk."

## Evidence Priority

Clinical diagnosis > laboratory results > long-term activity trend >
single-day activity.
