# Diabetes

## Definition

Diabetes is a chronic metabolic disease characterized by elevated blood
glucose levels resulting from problems with insulin production, insulin
action, or both.

## Types

The major types include:

- Type 1 diabetes
- Type 2 diabetes
- Gestational diabetes

## Why It Matters

Persistently elevated blood glucose can contribute to complications
affecting:

- cardiovascular system
- kidneys
- eyes
- nerves
- feet

## Relationship With Human Activity

Physical activity is an important component of maintaining metabolic and
cardiovascular health.

However, daily activity measurements cannot diagnose diabetes.

## Relevant Patient Data

The following information can be useful:

- blood glucose
- HbA1c
- weight
- blood pressure
- physical activity
- sleep
- medications
- medical history

## RAG Interpretation

The system should distinguish:

Known diabetes
from

Possible diabetes-related risk
from

General lifestyle observation.
