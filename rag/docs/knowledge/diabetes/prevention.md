# Diabetes Prevention

## Question: Can diabetes be prevented?

### Answer

Type 1 diabetes cannot currently be prevented by diet or exercise. Type 2 diabetes risk can often be reduced, not guaranteed eliminated, through physical activity, a healthier eating pattern, weight management when overweight, not smoking, and attention to blood pressure and other risk factors. Screening and gestational-diabetes follow-up also matter.

### Explanation

CDC and WHO describe type 2 diabetes as influenced by modifiable and non-modifiable factors. Family history and age cannot be changed. Prediabetes is a window for risk reduction, not a guarantee of future diabetes.

## Screening

People with symptoms, previous gestational diabetes, or multiple risk factors should ask a clinician about glucose or HbA1c testing. The e-doc diabetes model is not a screening laboratory.
