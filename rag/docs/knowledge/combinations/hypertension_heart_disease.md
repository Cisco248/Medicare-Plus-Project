# Hypertension and Heart Disease Together

High blood pressure makes the heart work harder and contributes to coronary disease, heart failure, and stroke. The heart-disease model does not take a BP reading; the hypertension model does not take smoking or stroke history. Clinically both matter. Emergency symptoms (chest pressure, severe breathlessness, collapse, FAST stroke signs) need emergency services.
