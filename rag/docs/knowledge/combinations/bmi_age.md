# BMI and Age Relationship

BMI screening categories for adults start at age 20 in CDC materials. Age and BMI both appear in the diabetes and hypertension models. Higher BMI at any adult age can be associated with cardiometabolic risk. Older age adds average cardiovascular risk even when BMI is in the healthy-weight band. The combination is not a diagnosis.
