# Blood Pressure and Age Relationship

Systolic pressure often rises with age in populations. AHA educational categories still use the same adult mmHg bands rather than a separate “age-adjusted diagnosis” in the public chart. Older age is a hypertension risk factor. The diabetes model uses both age and BP numbers. The hypertension model uses age but not the mmHg reading. One reading in an older adult still needs proper technique and repeats.
