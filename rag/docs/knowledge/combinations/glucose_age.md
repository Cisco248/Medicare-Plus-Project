# Glucose and Age Relationship

Age is a type 2 diabetes risk factor. Glucose is the measured sugar. Older age does not prove high glucose. Young people can have type 1 diabetes or other forms with very high glucose. The diabetes model uses both `age` and `glucose` after scaling. Neither pair is a diagnosis.
