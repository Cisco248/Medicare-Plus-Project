# Glucose and Blood Pressure Relationship

Diabetes and hypertension often occur together and together raise heart, stroke, and kidney risk. The diabetes model uses `glucose`, `systolic_bp`, `diastolic_bp`, and `hypertensive`. High glucose does not measure BP. High BP does not measure glucose. One high pair of numbers is not a dual diagnosis. The e-doc collects both because the trained diabetes model includes both, and because cardiometabolic care considers both.
