# Diabetes and Heart Disease Together

## Question: Why does diabetes increase cardiovascular risk?

### Answer

Persistently high glucose is associated with damage to blood vessels. Diabetes often travels with high blood pressure, unfavorable cholesterol, kidney disease, and obesity. These raise the chance of coronary heart disease, heart attack, and stroke.

## E-doc

The heart-disease form asks a survey diabetes status. The diabetes form does not output an ECG. Chest pain or stroke signs need emergency care regardless of either prediction.
