# BMI and Physical Activity Relationship

Physical inactivity is associated with type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular risk. BMI does not measure fitness. A higher BMI with regular activity is not identical to a higher BMI with sitting most of the day. Wearable steps cannot diagnose diabetes. WHO activity guidelines are population targets, not e-doc features. Only the heart-disease form includes mobility (`diff_walking`) and poor physical-health days, not a step count.
