# Hypertension Types and Measurement Concepts

## Primary versus secondary

Primary hypertension is the usual adult form. Secondary hypertension has another cause and needs clinical investigation when suggested by age, sudden onset, or other findings.

## Systolic and diastolic

Both numbers matter. Isolated systolic hypertension is common in older adults.

## Hypertensive emergency versus asymptomatic high readings

Emergency means extremely high readings plus acute organ-threat symptoms (chest pain, neurological deficit, breathlessness, confusion). Very high numbers without symptoms still need prompt medical advice but are not automatically labeled here as a specific mmHg emergency cutoff beyond AHA’s educational crisis-range discussion (>180 and/or >120) with symptoms.
