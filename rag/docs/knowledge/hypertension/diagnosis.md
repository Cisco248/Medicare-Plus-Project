# How Hypertension Is Identified

## Question: How is high blood pressure diagnosed?

### Answer

Hypertension is confirmed with repeated, correctly measured blood-pressure values and clinical assessment. NICE NG136 commonly uses a clinic reading of 140/90 mmHg or higher to prompt further measurement, often with home or ambulatory averages around 135/85 mmHg. AHA educational clinic categories start hypertension stage 1 at 130/80. These are diagnostic-pathway thresholds, not a single home reading and not the hypertension e-doc model output.

## Primary and secondary hypertension

Primary (essential) hypertension has no single identified cause. Secondary hypertension is due to another condition (for example kidney or endocrine disease). The app cannot distinguish them.

## Asymptomatic hypertension

Most people feel well. Feeling well does not prove BP is normal.

## Home monitoring

Use a validated upper-arm cuff, rest, correct cuff size, seated, feet on floor, arm at heart level. See monitoring knowledge.

## Model note

The hypertension classifier does not take mmHg as input. Identification of hypertension in clinic is separate from the e-doc label.
