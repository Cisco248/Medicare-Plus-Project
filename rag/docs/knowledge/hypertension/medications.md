# Hypertension Medications (Educational)

Common classes include ACE inhibitors, angiotensin-receptor blockers (ARBs), calcium-channel blockers, thiazide-like diuretics, and beta blockers. Purpose is to lower blood pressure and reduce stroke and heart-disease risk. Mechanisms differ by class. Side effects (cough, ankle swelling, dizziness, electrolyte changes) are possible and need clinical review. Adherence matters because hypertension is often silent. Never stop a prescribed antihypertensive without professional advice.

The hypertension model does not include a medication feature.
