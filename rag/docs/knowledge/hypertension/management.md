# Managing Hypertension

## Question: How can I lower blood pressure?

### Answer

Educational measures include reducing salt (WHO less than 5 g salt per day for adults unless a clinician advises otherwise), eating vegetables and fruits, being active as able, managing weight if overweight, limiting harmful alcohol, not smoking, sleeping adequately, and taking prescribed antihypertensives. Stress reduction can help wellbeing but does not replace measurement and medicines.

## Medicines

If a clinician prescribes tablets, they should be taken as directed even when the person feels well. Do not stop because an e-doc label looks reassuring.

## Follow-up

Home logs can support clinic visits. Targets are individual.
