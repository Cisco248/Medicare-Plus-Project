# Heart Disease Emergencies

Call emergency services for suspected heart attack, cardiac arrest (person unresponsive, not breathing normally), severe breathing difficulty, or stroke signs. Start CPR only if trained and instructed. Do not delay for RAG questions, e-doc resubmission, or wearable confirmation.
