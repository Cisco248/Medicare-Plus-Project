# Heart Disease Medications (Educational)

Classes commonly discussed in cardiovascular education include antiplatelet agents, statins and other lipid-lowering drugs, ACE inhibitors or ARBs, beta blockers, calcium-channel blockers, diuretics, anticoagulants when indicated, and anti-anginal drugs. Purpose is to reduce clot risk, improve lipids, lower blood pressure, control heart rate, or relieve symptoms depending on the class. Side effects and combinations are individual. Never stop a cardiac medicine because you feel well or because the model said lower risk.
