# Heart Disease Diet

Same pattern language as AHA and WHO: vegetables, fruits, whole grains, pulses, fish, nuts, seeds, modest dairy, limited salt, limited saturated and trans fat, limited sugary drinks. Sri Lankan examples: fish and lentil dishes, vegetables and greens, preferring less-fried cooking, watching salt in sambols and processed snacks, and moderating large refined-rice portions. No food is universally forbidden or curative.
