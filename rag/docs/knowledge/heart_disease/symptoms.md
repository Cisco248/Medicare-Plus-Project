# Heart Disease Symptoms

Chest pain, pressure, tightness, or burning; pain in the arm, neck, jaw, or back; shortness of breath; cold sweat; nausea; light-headedness; sudden collapse. Stroke signs are neurological (face, arm, speech). Palpitations and ankle swelling may occur in other heart conditions. Symptoms have many causes. Normal steps on a watch do not rule out a heart attack.
