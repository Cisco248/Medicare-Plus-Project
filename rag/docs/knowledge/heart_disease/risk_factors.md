# Heart Disease Risk Factors

Age, sex, smoking, diabetes (including borderline and pregnancy-related history in the survey field), high blood pressure, cholesterol, obesity/BMI, physical inactivity, unhealthy diet, family history, previous stroke, chronic kidney disease, and social conditions. The e-doc captures a subset: age band, sex, BMI, self-rated health, diabetic survey status, smoking, stroke, walking difficulty, and unhealthy days. Missing clinical BP or cholesterol on this form does not mean those risks are absent.
