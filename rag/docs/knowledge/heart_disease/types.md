# Types of Heart Disease

## Coronary heart disease / coronary artery disease

Narrowing of coronary arteries from atherosclerosis. Can cause angina or myocardial infarction (heart attack).

## Heart attack

An acute blockage. Emergency. Not diagnosed by the e-doc probability score.

## Heart failure

The heart’s pumping or filling is impaired. Breathlessness and swelling can occur. Needs clinical diagnosis.

## Arrhythmias

Irregular or abnormally fast or slow beats. Pulse-rate wearables can hint but cannot confirm the rhythm diagnosis.

## Valve disease and cardiomyopathy

Structural or muscle diseases. Imaging and specialist care are required.

The heart-disease model does not classify these types. It only emits high versus lower risk.
