# Heart Disease Diagnosis and Tests

Diagnosis uses clinical assessment plus tests such as ECG, blood biomarkers in acute care, echocardiography, stress testing, CT or invasive angiography, and risk scoring in clinic. The MediCare Plus model is not one of those tests. A high model probability may encourage a person to book clinical review; it does not replace it. A lower probability does not cancel emergency symptoms.
