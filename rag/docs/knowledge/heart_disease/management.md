# Heart Disease Management and Prevention

Educational pillars: do not smoke; be physically active as able after clinician clearance; eat a pattern rich in vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, fish, and unsaturated fats; limit salt, processed meat, and trans fat; take prescribed medicines; attend follow-up; know emergency symptoms. Cardiac rehabilitation is a clinical program. The chatbot must not prescribe exercise intensity or drug doses.
