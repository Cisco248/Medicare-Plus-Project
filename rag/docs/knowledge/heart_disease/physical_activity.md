# Heart Disease and Physical Activity

Regular activity supports cardiovascular health. WHO adult ranges apply when a clinician agrees. Chest pain, unexplained breathlessness, or fainting with exertion needs medical review, not a harder session. The model’s `DiffWalking` and `PhysicalHealth` features are survey items, not a workout plan.

## Sources

- WHO, Physical activity: <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity>
