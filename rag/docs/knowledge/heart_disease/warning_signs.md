# Heart Disease Warning Signs

Treat severe or persistent chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure, or sudden FAST stroke signs as emergencies. Do not wait for a second e-doc run. Mild palpitations without other features still deserve routine advice if they persist.
