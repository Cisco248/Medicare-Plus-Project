# High Cholesterol

## Definition

High cholesterol refers to unhealthy levels of blood lipids,
particularly LDL cholesterol, in the context of cardiovascular risk.

## Important Measurements

- total cholesterol
- LDL cholesterol
- HDL cholesterol
- triglycerides

## Symptoms

High cholesterol usually does not cause noticeable symptoms.

## Important Rule

The following cannot diagnose high cholesterol:

- low steps
- high steps
- calories
- sleep
- heart rate

A lipid test is required for appropriate assessment.

## Activity Relationship

Regular physical activity contributes to cardiovascular health and may
support healthy lipid profiles.

## RAG Interpretation

Use activity data as lifestyle context, not diagnostic evidence.
