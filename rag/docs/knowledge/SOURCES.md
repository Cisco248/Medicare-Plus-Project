## Source governance and update policy

Only named public-health agencies, national clinical-guideline bodies, and recognized professional health organizations are used. Secondary blogs, commercial wellness claims, anonymous material, and model-generated citations are excluded.

The knowledge base should be reviewed at least annually and sooner when a linked guideline is replaced, a recommendation materially changes, or a source URL becomes unavailable. Reviewers should verify the current page, publication or update date, applicability, and consistency with other authoritative guidance. Clinically meaningful changes require a version increment and changelog entry. Broken links may be replaced with the organization’s current equivalent without changing clinical meaning; substantive changes require content review.

Where organizations use different thresholds or targets, topic files avoid presenting one value as universally diagnostic. New exact values must include units, context, source, and limits. Emergency guidance takes precedence over lifestyle guidance.
