## Key Product Constraints
- Intended for patients registered through a government hospital clinic; clinical staff maintain the hospital data.
- The chatbot offers simple medical guidance only and does not replace a doctor.
- E-pharmacy purchases require doctor-prescription approval.
- Report uploads are currently limited to diabetes, hypertension, and heart-disease reports; unsupported reports must not be added to the hospital database.
