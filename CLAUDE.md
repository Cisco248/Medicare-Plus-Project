# Memory

## Projects
| Name | What |
|---|---|
| **Medicare Plus** | A government-hospital-connected Flutter Android application for registered clinical patients, with a backend, RAG system, human activity recognition (HAR), disease-condition models, and pharmacy support. |

## Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RAG** | Retrieval-augmented generation used to produce daily health summaries, recommendations, and chatbot answers from relevant medical context. |
| **HAR** | Human activity recognition engine/model that interprets mobile-health activity data and retains a latest-six-hour timeline. |

## Key Product Constraints
- Intended for patients registered through a government hospital clinic; clinical staff maintain the hospital data.
- The chatbot offers simple medical guidance only and does not replace a doctor.
- E-pharmacy purchases require doctor-prescription approval.
- Report uploads are currently limited to diabetes, hypertension, and heart-disease reports; unsupported reports must not be added to the hospital database.
